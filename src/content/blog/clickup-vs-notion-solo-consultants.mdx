This is one of the most-asked questions in solo-consultant communities, and most answers are really just "I use X and I like it." Here's a more useful framing: these tools were built for different jobs, and most solo consultants eventually want both — the question is which to start with.

## The core difference

**ClickUp is a task-and-project tool that also does docs.** Its strength is tracking work: tasks, due dates, statuses, time tracking, and dashboards. Everything is structured around "what needs to happen and by when."

**Notion is a docs-and-database tool that also does tasks.** Its strength is flexible knowledge management: proposal templates, SOPs, a client wiki, meeting notes that link to related pages. Everything is structured around "what do I know and how does it connect."

If your biggest pain point is "I forget to follow up on things" — start with ClickUp. If it's "I have no organized system for my templates, notes, and processes, everything's scattered across Google Docs" — start with Notion.

## Where each one wins for a consulting practice

**ClickUp wins for:**
- Tracking billable hours against projects (native time tracking)
- Client-facing task boards you can share a view of without giving full access
- Recurring task templates for repeatable engagements
- Dashboards that show you, at a glance, what's overdue across every active client

**Notion wins for:**
- A searchable library of proposal and contract templates
- Meeting notes that link directly to the client and project they relate to
- A lightweight internal wiki — your processes, pricing logic, and playbooks in one place
- Building a simple client-facing portal or shared page without extra tools

## The "use both" setup that actually works

A common, low-friction pattern: **ClickUp for anything with a deadline** (tasks, deliverables, invoicing follow-ups) and **Notion for anything you'd otherwise put in a Google Doc** (templates, SOPs, meeting notes, a wiki of client context). The two don't need to sync with each other for this to work at solo scale — you just need to be disciplined about which one is "home" for which kind of information.

## Our recommendation

If you can only run one tool right now: **ClickUp**, because missed deadlines cost you client trust faster than disorganized notes do. Add Notion once you notice you're recreating the same proposal or SOP from scratch more than twice — that's the signal your knowledge, not just your tasks, needs a home.

## FAQ

**Can I replace both with a simpler tool like Trello?**
For a few months, sure. Most solo consultants outgrow a single Kanban board once they're juggling more than 3–4 active clients with different deliverable types.

**Do either of these integrate with Zapier or Make?**
Yes, both have mature integrations with both automation platforms, so whichever project tool you pick, you can still automate onboarding, notifications, and reporting around it.
